Procedural generation keeps the build tiny instead of shipping 90 MB of assets — fast first load, low memory, and no install on low-end phones.

The state

One city, streamed block by block.

An 84 × 84 street grid spans 21.8 km per side. Only the blocks around you exist at any moment, so the map can stay enormous while the frame budget stays small.

Living streets

Traffic follows lanes and turns at intersections; pedestrians patrol sidewalks; police respond to your wanted level.

Interiors everywhere

Every building has a door and a procedural interior. Shops sell ammo and medkits, banks transfer and get robbed, dealerships and car meets move vehicles, trap houses pay passive income.

Vehicles that matter

Nine body classes rendered in detail, 4,200 named models with performance stats, plates and prices. Steal from traffic, summon from your garage, sell at the dealer.

Street economy

Cash, bank deposits, interest, five job types, car shows, passive businesses, wanted stars, hospital fees and persistent progress saved locally.

Engine

Optimized by design.

Instanced roads, props and traffic; merged facade geometry per block; adaptive resolution and draw distance; three quality presets with automatic fallback; mobile joystick, context buttons and touch look.

Streaming + instancing

Buildings merge into one draw call per facade style; lamps, trees, doors, glass and crosswalks are instanced.

Adaptive performance

Auto quality lowers resolution, draw distance and population when frame time rises, then recovers when there is headroom.

Responsive UI

Radar, objective tracker, weapon wheel, phone with map, garage, dealership, arms market, bank, jobs, assets, stats and system settings.

Single-file build

No asset downloads, no server logic — the game runs entirely in the browser with a CDN copy of Three.js.
